### Project Description
Digital Mirror is a multimodal AI-powered audit tool that acts as a "second pair of eyes" for your online brand. By analyzing social media bios, handles, and profile screenshots using Gemini 2.5 Flash, it quantifies your digital presence into professional metrics and provides immediate, actionable feedback to improve your employability and social standing.

### The Problem Statement
In today's digital age, your social media profile is your unofficial resume. Recruiters, clients, and peers often form a definitive first impression based on your "Digital Footprint." However, most individuals suffer from **Digital Blind Spots**—they are unaware that unprofessional handles, "red-flag" keywords, or inconsistent bio messaging are causing them to be filtered out by automated systems or human recruiters before they even get an interview.

### The Solution
We solve this by providing an objective, AI-driven "Mirror." Our platform uses a **Hybrid Audit Engine** to bridge the gap between self-perception and public perception. By combining high-speed local Regex heuristics with the deep visual and contextual reasoning of **Gemini 1.5 Flash**, the app identifies branding risks in real-time. It transforms raw, unpolished social data into a clean, executive dashboard that highlights exactly where a user stands and how they can "level up" their professional persona.

### Features
* **📷 Multimodal Analysis:** Seamlessly processes both raw text (Bio/Captions) and visual profile screenshots in a single audit.
* **🛡️ Hybrid Scoring:** Merges deterministic logic (Regex) for instant "red-flag" detection with probabilistic logic (AI) for deep tone analysis.
* **📊 Live Dashboarding:** Automatically parses AI responses into interactive doughnut charts visualizing Professionalism, Creativity, and Uniqueness.
* **💡 Strategic Roadmap:** Delivers custom-tailored advice, moving beyond generic tips to provide profile-specific branding strategies.
* **🎨 Dual-Tone UI:** A conceptual design journey that transitions from a "Sketchy" manual input style to a "Crisp" corporate dashboard style.
